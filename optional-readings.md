Each lecture has one or more "Your Choice" reading assignments.
During the semester, you **must** complete **at least two** "Your Choice" readings:
one before the first mid-term exam, and the other before the final exam.
Each exam will have one question about the "Your Choice" reading;
answering it correctly will be part of your grade on the exam.

You may be permitted to answer additional questions about other readings
for extra credit, too, so long as you don't get any incorrect. This incentivizes
you to read more than one "Your Choice" reading assignment.

One "Your Choice" reading ("What is Software Engineering"'s task related to reading
research paper abstracts) has a specific task associated with it. If you submit this
"Your Choice" assignment to Canvas before the final exam, you won't have to answer the
relevant question on the final.

## Honors Students

Students in the Honors section have an additional requirement related to these readings:
you must write a short reflection essay about the "Your Choice"
readings that you select. Writing one of these essays involves both reading the paper closely and trying
out one of its ideas yourself.

See the [Honors Reflection Essays page](../projects/honors-essays.html) for the full
assignment description, the deadlines, and the rubric.

If you are an Honors student, you should expect to have to answer a different (more in-depth and more difficult/time-consuming)
question about your chosen reading on the corresponding exam.
